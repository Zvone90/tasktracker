Book Your Tattoo

To arrange details and reserve a date as quickly as possible, send us:

  1. Your idea or description of what you want (feel free to attach references or photos of our work you like)
  2. Approximate size in centimeters (e.g. 10×15cm)
  3. Placement on the body
  4. Whether it's a first tattoo or a cover-up/rework

Once we've discussed the idea, we'll pick an available date together.
